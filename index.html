Villa Rimba sits on a quiet hillside above Lembang, where the air turns cool at dusk and the mornings begin with mist over the valley.

Built for families, couples and small groups who want privacy, comfort and nature — three calm bedrooms, an open living space that opens to the garden, and a private pool. No lobby, no crowds. Just your own house in the hills.
